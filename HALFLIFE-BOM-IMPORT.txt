HALF-LIFE ESSENTIAL PANEL BOM IMPORT
This draft lists 27 essential Seizo Screen entries, below the 80-item limit.
"Various capacitors" is one 33-piece PANEL lot; "Various resistors" is one 73-piece
PANEL lot. The exact panel factory BOM still covers all 150 fitted references.

Removed from this project's import: separate HAT fabrication/assembly/components,
optional USB hub/supply and controls, undefined independent arm-stop purchasing,
and shipping/tax/discount rows. Shipping/tax belong in Half-Life's separate box.
The matching HAT remains a required separately reconciled dependency; removing
its purchase lines does not make the original submitted HAT drop-in compatible.
Other system requirements remain documented in the detailed project files.

The two service rows use the preliminary PCBWay calculator amounts already
shown in the saved screenshot: $48.31 for one batch of five bare panels, and
$29.00 for assembly of one panel. Quantity is 1 for each. Their $77.31 sum
excludes component supply and is not a complete reviewed turnkey quotation.
These service prices remain subject to supplier review. Shipping stays separate.
The other 25 price cells remain 0.00 PENDING-QUOTE PLACEHOLDERS, not free parts.
Half-Life displays any zero as "no price". Real component costs are still needed
before treating this table as a complete funding budget.

Use Seizo Screen -> Parts -> Import from repo. Preview should show
00-bom-halflife.csv and 27 items. To remove an obsolete 69-item import, choose
Replace with 27 rather than Add; replacing clears the previous list.
This is also required to refresh prices on previously imported rows.
Alternatively edit just the two existing service prices using their pencils.
Repository changes may take up to 60 seconds to appear in the importer.

For PCBWay assembly, use assembly/BOM-PCBWay-panel.csv with the panel Gerbers
and placement CSV. This grouped draft is not a manufacturing placement BOM.
No circuit, routing, selected exact component or fabrication export was changed.
