ASSEMBLY PROCESS EXCEPTIONS THAT REQUIRE FACTORY ACCEPTANCE
- Panel C25 is the sole bottom component: THT ceramic, not SMT. Body at least
  1.5mm above rear PCB surface; nearby clipped leads at most1.0mm; no spikes.
- Panel U6 is a COMPLETE Adafruit4683 regulator breakout with its header
  installed. Not just a header and not the bare MPM3610 chip. Pad1 GND,
  pad2 3.3V OUT, pad3 5V VIN, pad4 EN left unconnected. At native coordinates
  these pads run (141.40,152), (143.94,152), (146.48,152), (149.02,152)mm.
  The header footprint does not represent the module body. Confirm the actual
  module body direction/stand-off against neighbouring parts before soldering;
  planning envelope is10.16 x17.145mm, height6.5mm. No unreviewed inversion.
- U4 TO92 holes are intentionally1.0mm; D2 LED holes1.1mm. Use native files,
  not replacement stock footprints. Keep LED stamping shoulders above PCB.
- Meter-divider R52/R53 are49.9k0.1%; R54 is10k0.1%, all exact CMF55 parts
  on10.16mm pitch. Ordinary1% substitutes are not approved.
- U8/U9/U10 must be SN74LV125AN; U11 SN74AHC14N; U5 TPS2553DBVR-1.
  Generic HC buffers or a different current-switch suffix are not substitutes.
- Header BOM rows specify finished connector lengths. The factory may cut
  same-family PRPC040SAAN-RC strips; the DigiKey list's two strips are raw stock,
  not two fitted connectors. Total finished panel connectors:13, contacts:49.
- HAT U3/U4/U5 are SN74LVC1G17DBVR buffers. Never fit the original AP2112.
  HAT C7 and R10 are absent from the board. Schematic-only C1/R1/R2/U1/JP1
  are deliberately DNP/board-excluded; do not add an EEPROM circuit to this order.
  J1 tall stacking socket is on the BOTTOM.
  USB J8 has SMT signal contacts AND soldered through-hole shield tabs.
  ESP32 U2 is SMT; its plated thermal vias do not make it an inserted DIP.
  Factory must review open0.30mm thermal-via paste wicking/stencil process.
- The HAT F1 BOM entry is its fuse HOLDER. Its insertable fuse is separately
  listed under off-board items and has a provisional motor-load rating.
- Do not change package, tolerance, supply specifications, footprint, pinout or
  module variant without an engineering review of the proposed substitution.

PLACEMENT COORDINATES
The CPL files retain KiCad10 native machine-export convention: mm, original
origin, X unchanged, Y=-native-board-Y, no additional bottom X mirroring.
Rotation is the native exported rotation. All-component CPL files include THT
for manual insertion reference. SMT-placements files include HAT USB hybrid
contacts and the ESP32; the shield tabs require a separate solder operation.
Assembly drawings use front view and MIRRORED bottom view. The pin/net map
uses absolute native coordinates with positive Y downward. Do not mix those
two Y conventions. Supplier must confirm machine rotation and pin1 visually.

DISPLAY / FULL SYSTEM REMAINING WORK
Waveshare16446 has both HX8347D and ST7789 hardware variants. The supplier
must identify the actual unit and controller. Panel J11 requires its INTERNAL
regulated3V3 rail, which the11-pin display connector does not expose. The
actual tap point and insulated wire attachment must be qualified on the actual
display before claiming a completely solder-free, plug-in complete assembly.
No generic substitute for that tap or assumption of a particular batch is valid.
Use ASSEMBLY-CONNECTIONS.txt and CONNECTOR-PIN-MAP.csv for the other harnesses.
The board's HOLD/stop-status inputs do not cut motor power independently.
Independent dual-supply stopping and gravity retention remain separate unfinished
work; all reported non-base arm joints can drop without motor power.
