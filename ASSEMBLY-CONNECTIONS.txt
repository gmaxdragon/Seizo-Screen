SEIZO SCREEN REV23 — ENGINEERING ASSEMBLY CONNECTIONS
These describe a first prototype; they are not evidence of an assembled or qualified product.

1. Keep motor supplies disconnected and the arm mechanically supported during panel bring-up.
   Use a working iron, appropriate small-SMD assembly help and a multimeter. No-force sample
   fit exact parts before soldering. BOM notes preserve nominal-only lead-fit uncertainties.
2. Use the exact selected BOM. Orient DIP notches/pin1, LED polarities, electrolytics and
   SOT-23 pin1 from their drawings and native assembly artwork. Avoid generic HC125 parts:
   U8/U9/U10 are SN74LV125AN; U11 is SN74AHC14N. Current switch is TPS2553DBVR-1.
3. C25 is the sole bottom part. Keep its body >=1.5 mm off the rear and neighbouring clipped
   leads <=1.0 mm; no solder spikes. It lies outside the Pi/HAT board envelope.
4. The Adafruit4683 converter physical header is panel U6: 1 GND, 2 3.3V OUT, 3 VIN, 4 EN.
   Leave EN unconnected; the module already pulls it up. Use insulated module clearance.
   Preserve the dedicated 1 mm copper feed and short paired 22AWG J13 power/ground harness.
5. Assemble ONLY the matching-HAT revision from its own files. Its U3 is SN74LVC1G17DBVR,
   not AP2112. Never install that buffer in the original regulator PCB or parallel regulators.
   Detailed HAT changes and DNP C7 are in matching-HAT/HAT-REV23-ASSEMBLY.md.

POWER AND HARNESS (check pin1 at BOTH ends; view direction can reverse apparent order):
Panel J7.1 <- matching HAT J12.1 5V; panel J7.2 <- HAT J12.2 GND. J7.3 unused.
Panel J13.1 -> HAT J10.1 GND; J13.2 -> HAT J10.2 BOARD_3V3; dedicated 22AWG pair.
Panel J1 pins1..6 -> HAT J7 pins1..6: SCL,SDA,IRQ,ADC_CS,3V3,GND.
Panel J5 pins1..6 -> HAT J6 pins1..6: MISO,MOSI,SCLK,LCD_CS,3V3,GND.
Panel J6 pins1..6 -> HAT J5 pins1..6: LCD_DC,LCD_RST,LCD_BL,TP_CS,3V3,GND.
Panel J12.1 -> HAT J4.3 (ESP32 GPIO6 LCD_VIO_PRESENT).
Panel J12.2 -> HAT J4.4 (ESP32 GPIO7 BB_FAULT_N). Common ground is mandatory.
All pin numbers/nets also appear in the generated CONNECTOR-PIN-MAP.csv.

LCD: Waveshare16446 2.8inch SPI resistive touch, 320x240. Board about79.85x50.8mm;
visible area57.6x43.2mm. It is a status/touch interface, not a Pi desktop/video USB monitor.
Panel J2.1..11 -> actual labelled LCD 5V,GND,MISO,MOSI,SCLK,LCD_CS,DC,RST,BL,TP_CS,TP_IRQ.
Panel J11.1 must reach the LCD's actual INTERNAL regulated3.3V rail; J11.2 is ground.
The 11-pin LCD connector does not expose this rail. Actual supplied module revision/tap
must be checked before wire attachment. Do not substitute panel3V3 for actual LCD_VIO.
The old published module has AMS1117 VOUT at pin2/tab; that is only a candidate until the
actual ST7789/HX8347D board is identified and measured. A controller-specific reference build
is included; there is no automatic controller identification through this header.

MOUNTING: Pi4 -> matching HAT: selected tall socket planning gap15.5mm, verify seating.
HAT front -> panel rear: 30mm CLEAR gap. Panel front -> LCD rear: 25mm spacers.
Panel is cable-connected and mechanically supported; it has no new40pin stacking connector.
LCD mounting coordinates in panel absolute mm: (62.475,57.5),(137.375,57.5),
(62.475,103.3),(137.375,103.3). Use M2.5 screws, washer/head contact OD<=5mm.
Other mounting hardware contact OD<=7mm at H1-H4; explicit copper exclusions are present.
Do not infer safe screw lengths from nominal gap: check engagement/protrusion physically.
Use the board-only STEP for PCB geometry; it omits all components, cables, fan and enclosure.
Fan model/current and full exact mating harness are unknown; clearance is not guaranteed.
Breadboard occupied area is about46x36x10mm; it mounts in the labelled reserved rectangle.

NORMAL PI LINK: existing Pi header UART through the revised HAT buffers: Pi physical8 TX
to ESP GPIO44 RX, ESP GPIO43 TX to Pi physical10 RX. Only3.3V logic with common ground.
Use the UART reference build for this topology and explicitly configure the actual Pi port.
USB service uses the existing HAT USB-C connector and a normal data cable. That connector
does NOT supply screen/HAT operating power. The economy design has no fitted panel USB-C
extension, custom five-port hub or Bluetooth firmware. Keep HAT USB access unobstructed.
Reuse a hub only after its total load/power states are qualified. Priced preferred candidate
is Raspberry Pi SC1636 powered hub plus its own official US supply ($23.05 before delivery).
The existing7port hub is bus-powered. PiUSB total current is1.2A; more sockets do not add power.

FEATURES: ten customizable button inputs, HOLD request, expander reset, two dry-NC stop-status
inputs, touch display, switchable common-ground0-24V DC voltage sensing and protected5V
breadboard branch. Button-to-arm action integration is unfinished. J3/J4 are DRY CONTACTS
ONLY, not motor-power terminals. NC closed toGND is healthy; open asserts a stop request.
The screen/panel/Pi cannot provide the requested independent arm emergency-stop function.
Breadboard J10: pin1 protected5V, pin2GND, intended100mA usable load; no motors or other
external supply injection. SW14 OFF thenON resets its hardware latch after removing fault.
Measured short-circuit behavior and shared-rail brownouts remain prototype tests.
Meter J9: pin1probe+, pin2COMMON GND. Only known common-ground0-24V DC; no current,
resistance, mains or arbitrary unknown circuits. It is not an isolated commercial multimeter.
Display meter data remains disabled until enabled/configured; calibration is unverified.

FIRST PROTOTYPE VALIDATION ORDER: inspect and continuity-check unpowered board; check all
rails for shorts; start with motors and external peripherals disconnected; use controlled
low-voltage power and measure5V/3V3/no backfeed; then connect/verify LCD actual-VIO and harness;
verify inputs/display/touch/NC polarity and disconnected-wire states; check breadboard cutoff;
calibrate meter against a trusted instrument; exercise communication/power-loss/reconnect
and real combined load. Keep actuator bindings absent until independent stopping, retention
and mechanical integrity are resolved. No live drop experiment is part of this procedure.
